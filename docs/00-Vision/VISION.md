# Automation OS

## Vision

To build an Automation Operating System that enables individuals and organizations to transform ideas into intelligent, executable workflows.

---

## Mission

Create a flexible platform where reusable capabilities can be composed into workflows without rewriting business logic.

The platform should evolve with technology instead of being tied to it.

---

## Why do we exist?

Modern businesses spend enormous time repeating processes.

Our mission is to transform repetitive knowledge work into reusable automation.

We don't automate tasks.

We automate business thinking.

---

## What problem are we solving?

Businesses have hundreds of disconnected tools.

Every new process requires building custom software again.

Automation should be modular, reusable and intelligent.

---

## What are we NOT building?

We are NOT building:

- A video editor
- An AI model
- A social media platform
- A workflow hardcoded for one business

Those are applications built on top of the platform.

---

## Core Principles

Business First

Domain Before Technology

Composition over Duplication

Plugins over Hardcoding

Automation First

Intelligence as a Capability

Everything Evolves

---

## Long Term Vision

Automation OS becomes a platform where developers and businesses create intelligent workflow applications by assembling reusable capabilities.

---

## Success Criteria

Someone can create an entirely new automation product without changing the Core Engine.
